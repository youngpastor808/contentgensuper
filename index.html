Legal & Privacy Center

ContentGen AI Studio • Last updated: March 2026

Privacy Commitment Summary: ContentGen AI is built with an offline-first, client-isolated architecture. Your stories, media assets, audio recordings, and personal API keys remain securely on your local device or encrypted cloud sessions. We do not sell your personal data or train public foundation models on your private storyboards.

1. Information We Collect

When using ContentGen AI Director Studio, we collect only the minimal data necessary to fulfill video generation, storyboarding, and audio synthesis features:

  • User Account Information: If you sign in via Google or Email authentication, we store your user identifier (UID), email address, and credit balance in our secure Firebase database.
  • Storyboard Prompts & Scripts: Narrative text, scene dialogues, directorial camera directions, and visual descriptors submitted for generation.
  • Uploaded Media: Custom reference images, voice recordings, and background music tracks provided for scene generation or audio synchronization.
  • User-Supplied API Keys: Custom Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, ElevenLabs, Deepgram, Stability AI, or OpenAI Gateway credentials configured in your workspace settings.

2. How We Process Data with AI Providers

To produce cinematic imagery, videos, scripts, and synthesized speech, ContentGen AI sends request payloads to authorized AI model endpoints based on your selected settings:

  • Google Gemini & Vertex AI: Story expansion, character generation, scene decomposition, and image generation (Gemini 3.x, 2.5, Nano Banana, Veo 3.1). Data sent through Google APIs is governed by Google Cloud / Google AI Studio privacy policies.
  • Deepgram: High-precision speech-to-text audio alignment and word timestamp extraction.
  • ElevenLabs & Gradio WebAudio: Text-to-speech voice synthesis.
  • Stability AI & Pollinations: Image generation fallback pipelines.

3. Client-Side API Key Privacy & Isolation

All personal API keys (Google AI Studio keys, Vertex credentials, third-party provider tokens) entered in your Settings are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage). When requests pass through our backend proxy server, keys are transmitted solely as pass-through authorization headers and are never stored, logged, or retained on our servers.

4. Local Persistence & Project Data

ContentGen AI uses browser IndexedDB (ContentGenAI_DB) to store your storyboard clips, generated image blobs, video keyframes, audio timelines, and undo history locally on your computer. You can export complete standalone project JSON archives or wipe the local database at any time from the Project Management menu.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, storyboards, or generated media with third-party advertisers. Data is shared strictly with the infrastructure providers required to execute your requested AI generation tasks.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Export your entire storyboard, timeline, audio clips, and images to local ZIP or JSON files.
  • Clear cached transcripts and IndexedDB storage in one click from the Settings panel.
  • Request complete deletion of your user account and cloud project saves by contacting support.

7. Contact Information

For questions or privacy inquiries regarding ContentGen AI, please reach out to the project creator via @jonesmbuso.
